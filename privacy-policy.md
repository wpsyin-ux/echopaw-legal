# EchoPaw 隐私政策 / Privacy Policy

**生效日期 / Effective Date:** 2026-10-20

## 中文版本

### 1. 我们是谁
EchoPaw 是一款宠物陪伴与记录应用，提供宠物日记、健康档案、场景情绪演示和成长回顾功能。

### 2. 本地保存的信息
当前版本将以下信息保存在你设备上的应用本地存储中，应用本身不主动将这些内容上传到我们的服务器：

- 宠物日记：文字、日期和选择的照片
- 健康档案：宠物基本信息、体重、疫苗、驱虫和皮肤观察记录（包括选择的照片）
- 场景情绪演示：选择的场景、模拟结果和反馈；应用不录音，也不分析真实声音
- 应用设置：例如语言偏好

设备操作系统提供的备份或同步功能可能会处理应用本地数据，具体取决于你的设备设置。

### 3. 照片
你从相册选择的照片会被复制到应用的本地目录，用于展示日记和皮肤观察记录。当前版本不会将这些照片上传到我们的服务器。应用不会删除你在系统相册中的原始照片。

### 4. 演示结果与健康提示
狗狗情绪结果仅基于所选场景和规则生成，**不访问麦克风，不录制或分析音频**，也不是狗语翻译。皮肤观察建议仅基于你选择的症状，不会分析照片。所有健康内容仅供日常观察参考，**不能替代兽医诊断**。

### 5. 第三方服务
当前版本不包含广告、分析或跨应用追踪 SDK，也不进行跨应用追踪。

### 6. 删除与保留
应用内“设置 → 清空全部记录”会清空日记、健康记录和场景演示记录，但会保留宠物基本信息、语言偏好和已复制到应用目录的照片文件。删除单条记录也不会自动清理其本地照片副本。若要移除应用保存的全部本地数据，可以卸载应用；卸载后日记等记录可能无法恢复。卸载应用不会删除你系统相册中的原始照片。

### 7. 联系方式
如需应用支持或咨询隐私问题，请发送邮件至 [plhjzyyx@163.com](mailto:plhjzyyx@163.com)。

## English Version

### 1. Who We Are
EchoPaw is a pet companion and journaling app with diaries, health records, scene-based emotion demos, and growth reviews.

### 2. Information Stored Locally
The current version stores the following information in the app’s local storage on your device. The app itself does not actively upload this content to our servers:

- Pet diary entries: text, dates, and selected photos
- Health records: pet profile details, weight, vaccinations, deworming and skin-observation records (including selected photos)
- Scene-based emotion demos: selected scenes, simulated results and feedback; the app does not record or analyze real audio
- App settings, such as language preference

Your device’s operating-system backup or sync features may process local app data according to your device settings.

### 3. Photos
Photos selected from your library are copied into the app’s local directory to display diaries and skin-observation records. The current version does not upload these photos to our servers. The app does not delete the original photos in your system photo library.

### 4. Demo Results and Health Guidance
Dog-emotion results are generated from the selected scene and rules only. They **do not access the microphone, record or analyze audio**, and they are not translations of dog language. Skin-observation guidance is based on symptoms you select, not on photo analysis. Health information is for everyday observation only and **is not a substitute for veterinary diagnosis**.

### 5. Third-Party Services
The current version has no advertising, analytics or cross-app tracking SDKs and does not perform cross-app tracking.

### 6. Deletion and Retention
“Settings → Clear All Records” clears diary, health and scene-demo records, but retains pet profile details, the language preference and photo files copied into the app’s local directory. Deleting an individual record does not automatically remove its local photo copy either. To remove all locally stored app data, you can uninstall the app; local records may not be recoverable after uninstalling. Uninstalling does not delete original photos in your system photo library.

### 7. Contact
For app support or privacy questions, email [plhjzyyx@163.com](mailto:plhjzyyx@163.com).
