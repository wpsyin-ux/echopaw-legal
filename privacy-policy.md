# EchoPaw 隐私政策 / Privacy Policy

**生效日期 / Effective Date:** 2026-10-27

## 中文版本

### 1. 我们是谁
EchoPaw 是一款宠物陪伴与记录应用，提供宠物日记、健康档案和成长回顾功能。使用 EchoPaw 无需注册账号。

### 2. 保存在你设备上的信息
你创建的以下内容保存在你设备上的应用本地存储中。应用本身不会将这些内容上传到我们的服务器，我们也无法访问它们：

- 宠物日记：文字、日期和你选择的照片
- 宠物资料：名字（必填）、种类、品种、性别和生日（选填），均由你自行填写
- 健康档案：体重、疫苗、驱虫和皮肤观察记录（包括你选择的照片）
- 应用设置：语言偏好，以及每日提醒的开关和提醒时间

设备操作系统提供的备份或同步功能（例如 iCloud 备份）可能会处理应用的本地数据，具体取决于你的设备设置。

### 3. 照片
你从系统相册选择的照片会被复制到应用的本地目录，用于展示日记和皮肤观察记录。选择照片时，系统可能会请求相册访问权限。EchoPaw 只使用你选中的照片，不会扫描或读取你没有选择的照片；应用不会把这些照片上传到我们的服务器，也不会删除你在系统相册中的原始照片。

### 4. 每日提醒
每日提醒是可选功能，默认关闭。只有当你在“设置”中打开它时，应用才会向系统申请通知权限。提醒由你的手机在你设定的时间于本机发出，不需要联网，也不会向我们发送任何信息。你可以随时在应用的“设置”或系统设置中关闭。

### 5. 健康提示
健康档案中的皮肤观察建议仅依据你选择的症状按固定规则生成，不会分析照片。成长回顾是根据你记录的内容按固定规则生成的。所有这些内容仅供日常观察和回顾参考，**不能替代兽医诊断**。如果宠物出现严重症状，请及时咨询兽医。

### 6. 第三方服务与链接
EchoPaw 不包含广告、统计分析或跨应用追踪的第三方 SDK，也不进行跨应用追踪。

应用“关于”页面中的“隐私政策”和“使用支持”链接会在系统浏览器中打开，这些网页由 GitHub Pages 托管。访问网页时，GitHub 可能会按其自身的隐私政策处理访问信息（例如 IP 地址）；这发生在 EchoPaw 之外。

### 7. 删除与保留
- 应用内“设置 → 清空全部记录”会清空日记和健康记录，但会保留宠物资料、应用设置和已复制到应用目录的照片文件。
- 删除单条日记不会自动清理它的本地照片副本。
- 如需移除应用保存的全部本地数据，可以卸载应用；卸载后日记等记录可能无法恢复。卸载应用不会删除你系统相册中的原始照片。

### 8. 联系方式
如需应用支持或咨询隐私问题，请发送邮件至 [plhjzyyx@163.com](mailto:plhjzyyx@163.com)。我们仅会使用你的邮箱地址和你在邮件中提供的内容来回复你的问题。

### 9. 政策更新
如果本政策有变化，我们会更新本页面和上方的生效日期。

## English Version

### 1. Who We Are
EchoPaw is a pet companion and journaling app with diaries, health records and growth reviews. You do not need to create an account to use EchoPaw.

### 2. Information Stored on Your Device
The following content that you create is stored in the app’s local storage on your device. The app itself does not upload it to our servers, and we cannot access it:

- Pet diary entries: text, dates and the photos you select
- Pet profile: name (required), and species, breed, gender and birthday (optional), all entered by you
- Health records: weight, vaccinations, deworming and skin-observation records (including photos you select)
- App settings: language preference, and the daily reminder switch and time

Your device’s operating-system backup or sync features (for example, iCloud Backup) may process local app data according to your device settings.

### 3. Photos
Photos you select from your photo library are copied into the app’s local directory to display diaries and skin-observation records. When you choose photos, the system may ask for photo library access. EchoPaw only uses the photos you select and does not scan or read photos you did not choose. The app does not upload these photos to our servers and does not delete the original photos in your photo library.

### 4. Daily Reminder
The daily reminder is optional and off by default. The app asks the system for notification permission only when you turn it on in Settings. The reminder is shown by your phone, on the device, at the time you choose. It does not need an internet connection and sends nothing to us. You can turn it off at any time in the app’s Settings or in system settings.

### 5. Health Guidance
Skin-observation tips in the health file are generated by fixed rules from the symptoms you select; photos are not analyzed. Growth reviews are generated by fixed rules from what you recorded. All of this is for everyday observation and reflection only and **is not a substitute for veterinary diagnosis**. If your pet shows serious symptoms, please consult a veterinarian.

### 6. Third-Party Services and Links
EchoPaw contains no advertising, analytics or cross-app tracking SDKs and does not perform cross-app tracking.

The “Privacy Policy” and “Support” links on the app’s About page open in your system browser. Those pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. When you visit them, GitHub may process access information (such as your IP address) under its own privacy policy; this happens outside EchoPaw.

### 7. Deletion and Retention
- “Settings → Clear All Records” clears diary and health records, but keeps your pet profile, app settings and photo files already copied into the app’s local directory.
- Deleting an individual diary entry does not automatically remove its local photo copy.
- To remove all locally stored app data, you can uninstall the app; local records may not be recoverable after uninstalling. Uninstalling does not delete original photos in your system photo library.

### 8. Contact
For app support or privacy questions, email [plhjzyyx@163.com](mailto:plhjzyyx@163.com). We use your email address and the content of your message only to reply to you.

### 9. Changes to This Policy
If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
